# Forecasting an opponent's next two picks

## Decisions fixed before confirmation

Compare three shortlists of up to three unordered champion pairs at the three consecutive-pick windows, after 7, 9 and 17 completed actions. All three see the same first-pick team, legal champions, current prefix, previous games’ Fearless locks and earlier completed tournament series. The role-first candidate predicts the two roles first and marginalizes uncertain champion-to-role assignments. The existing private model ranks observed pairs alongside pairs retrieved from its draft tree. The simple baseline ranks earlier legal co-occurrences on the same patch, reverting to all older patches when patch history is empty. Ties in the frequency baseline break by canonical champion name.

Fit the candidate using only GRID series completed through April 30, 2026. Use the 599 previously examined games for development diagnostics, including integration and leakage checks. Freeze candidate parameters, implementation and evidence before scoring new games. Reserve all four 2026 Korean postseason stages: LCK Season Play-In and Playoffs, and LCK CL Season Play-In and Playoffs (106 indexed games). Validate explicit first-pick identity, patch, chronological actions, complete Fearless series and no overlaps with previous data. Keep missing played roles out of role-label metrics and disclose coverage. Role labels and target-game roster information cannot enter forecasts. No replacement stages or model changes after seeing the scores.

For each method report exact unordered-pair Hit@1 and Hit@3, raw counts, mean reciprocal rank truncated at three, and by league and window. Report role-pair top-three accuracy separately where both played positions can be verified. Pair rank scores do not represent probability. For role-first minus each comparator use 5,000 paired bootstrap samples of whole series, stratified by competition, seed 10429. A positive lower endpoint in **both** 95% intervals is the criterion to consider a later serving trial. All remaining decisions keep the existing coach-facing model. These historical games provide a retrospective transfer test. The diagnostic baseline in previous work is post hoc.

## Prospective comparison

Freeze a separate private trial before a real eligible fixture. Record the models, evidence, event, patch and champion pool. Publish no licensed predictions or inputs. Keep a game commitment distinct from the forecasts made when actual prefixes are known. For forecasts made before the draft, use the same 32 simulated legal prefixes per window for all three models, with seed 10429. Generate actions from the dated, frozen one-step forecaster and aggregate a model’s ranked pairs by the sum of reciprocal ranks across simulations. Record the three highest-scoring pairs per window with deterministic name tiebreaks. Score these unconditional shortlists separately from the live conditional shortlists. Rehearsals, retrospective predictions, missing windows, unverifiable timing and cancellations must remain identifiable. Only count live hits with a matching observed prefix and independently verifiable timing. A pilot is evidence that logging works; decisions on a future release require adequate series coverage.
